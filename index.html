What this is

Leadership is a path, not a title.

Lead to Leave is a three-stage system that moves you from carrying everything yourself to building something that runs — and grows — without you. First you learn to lead yourself, then to lead through others, then to build leaders who no longer need you. Every leader is somewhere on that path. This audit finds where you are right now, so you begin with the work that will actually move you forward.

The map

Three stages of leadership

LIFTINGYou're carrying the weight. Everything runs through you; when you're gone, things slip. This stage is the climb out.
LEADINGYou're building people — developing, coaching, holding a higher standard. Harder and slower than doing it yourself. That's the point.
LEAVINGYou've built something that runs without you. People grow and own outcomes — they could go anywhere, and they stay.
