The CORE Audit
Before you buy a single thing — where do you actually lead from?

Be honest with yourself for five minutes. Not where you want to be — where you actually are, today, with the team in front of you. This sorts you into one of three stages and names the Foundations to work first. There's no judgment here; every leader I've worked with started somewhere, and most started further back than they thought.

The three stages

LIFTING — you're carrying the weight.
Everything runs through you — the work, the decisions, the problems. The team functions because you're present. When you're gone, things slip. You might call it "being hands-on." I'd call it being the process.
  • You end most days drained, your own priorities untouched
  • You carry the problems home — it's hard to switch off
  • Time off feels risky, so you rarely take it
  • A quiet worry that if you stepped away, it would all unravel
LEADING — you're building your people.
You've started shifting from doing to developing — having the conversations, setting expectations, coaching through mistakes instead of fixing them yourself. Harder and slower than doing it yourself. That's exactly the point.
  • You fight the urge to grab the wheel and just do it yourself
  • Some weeks feel like real progress, others like backsliding
  • Wins are starting to come from your people, not only you
  • You're learning to sit with the discomfort of letting them struggle
LEAVING — you've built something that doesn't need you.
Your team runs when you're not there. People are growing, owning outcomes. They could get hired anywhere — they stay because of what you've built. That's Lead to Leave.
  • You’ve proven the team can run without you — now you want that true every week, not just the good ones
  • You’re ready to build leaders who build other leaders, not just strong individuals
  • Stepping back further still triggers a quiet “but what if” you haven’t fully silenced
  • Freedom, scale, or an exit is on your mind — and you want what you built to hold without you
